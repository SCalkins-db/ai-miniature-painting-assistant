Painters everywhere are making the switch to 
Warpaints Fanatic by The Army Painter. To help with 
this transition, we’ve created a handy conversion chart.
This chart enables you to find the closest matches 
between Warpaint Fanatic, our original Warpaints-
range, and Warpaints Air, as well as paints from other 
popular manufacturers.
The suggestions in this chart provide close colour 
matches, however they are not always exact, but close 
enough for even the most discerning gamer.
Good luck with your colour conversion!
